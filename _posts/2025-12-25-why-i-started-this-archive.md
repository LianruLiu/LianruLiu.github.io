This archive was created for one simple reason:

To think clearly in public.

Most people live reactively.  
They consume noise, follow urgency, and forget what they once believed.

I do not want to live that way.

I want a place where ideas can be written down, refined, challenged, and remembered.

A place where progress becomes visible.

This archive is not a diary.

It is a record of development.

It contains thoughts on:

- economics  
- wealth  
- technology  
- ambition  
- discipline  
- books  
- mistakes  
- growth

Some entries may be short.  
Some may be unfinished.  
Some may later prove wrong.

That is acceptable.

Because clarity is built through iteration.

Years from now, I want to look back and see evidence of evolution.

Not comfort.  
Not excuses.  
Evolution.

If others find value here, that is welcome.

But first, this archive is a contract with myself.

To keep learning.  
To keep building.  
To keep becoming.

— Lianru Liu
